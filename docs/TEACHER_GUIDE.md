# Teacher guide

## Joining your school
1. Click **Register**, create an account and verify your email.
2. Choose **I am a teacher joining my school**. Pick your state, local government and school.
3. Choose your roles: class teacher for a class and arm, and/or the subjects and classes you teach.
4. Send the request. You see nothing until your school admin approves you. The admin may change your roles.

## Attendance (class teachers)
Open **Attendance**. Use the arrows or type a date (DD/MM/YYYY). Switch AM and PM on for students who are present, or use **All AM**, **All PM** and **Clear day**. It saves by itself and works without internet; changes sync later. **Weekly** and **Term** show percentages and who is **At risk**. Both can be exported as CSV.

## Students
Class teachers can add students to their own class and arm, one at a time or from a filled template (**Students > Download template**, then **Paste or import list**). They show as **Pending** until the admin confirms. The app warns about likely duplicates.

## Scores (subject teachers)
1. Open **Scores** and choose a class and subject at the top.
2. Pick the term. Type marks; limits are enforced and **Enter** moves to the next student. "Saved ✓" confirms saving.
3. The bonus marks (attendance, textbook, notebook) are added to the exam when **Add bonus marks to exam** is ticked. The exam never goes above its maximum.
4. **Report** shows one student's subject report. **Class > Export to Excel** makes the paper scoresheet layout.

You can edit only your own subjects. Class teachers and admins can read every subject for their class.

## Report cards
Class teachers open **Reports** for combined report cards and **Print all report cards**.
