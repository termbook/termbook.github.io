# Teacher guide

## Joining your school
1. Click **Register**, create an account and verify your email.
2. Choose **I am a teacher joining my school**. Pick your state, local government and school.
3. Choose your roles. Class teacher: pick your one class and arm. Subject teacher: open each subject you teach and tick only the classes (and arms) where you teach it. JSS and SS have different subjects, so each subject only offers the classes where it is taught. For example, tick Basic Science for JSS3, and Mathematics for every SS2 arm, without ticking Mathematics for JSS3.
4. Send the request. You see nothing until your school admin approves you. The admin may change your roles.

## Attendance (class teachers)
Open **Attendance**. Use the arrows or type a date (DD/MM/YYYY). Switch AM and PM on for students who are present, or use **All AM**, **All PM** and **Clear day**. It saves by itself and works without internet; changes sync later. **Weekly** and **Term** show percentages and who is **At risk**. Both can be exported as CSV.

## Students
Class teachers can add students to their own class and arm with the full form (student, parent or guardian, father and mother, emergency contact, health and other details), or from a filled template (**Students > Download template**, then **Paste or import list**). They show as **Pending** until the admin confirms. Class teachers can also correct the names, admission numbers and details of students in their own class: open the student and change it. Tell the parent if you change an admission number they already use. Each change is recorded in the audit log. Subject teachers cannot edit students. The app warns about likely duplicates.

## Scores (subject teachers)
1. Open **Scores**. Choose the **class** first, then the **subject** (the subject list shows only your subjects in that class).
2. Pick the term. The top shows how many students are scored, the class average, the highest and lowest totals, and the grades.
3. Each student is one row. **Tap a student** to open their entry sheet: 1st CA, 2nd CA, open day, exam and the three bonus marks, with the total and grade updating as you type. Everything saves by itself. Use **Next** and **Previous** to move through the class, or **Done** to close. On a keyboard, **Enter** moves to the next box and, after the last box, to the next student.
4. A green edge on a row means all four main scores are in; amber means some are. Use **Male**, **Female** and **Not scored yet** to narrow the list, or search by name.
5. The bonus marks are added to the exam when **Add bonus marks to exam** is ticked. The exam never goes above its maximum.
6. **Results** shows the ranking for one term or the whole year and can be exported. **Class > Export to Excel** makes the paper scoresheet layout.

You can edit only your own subjects. Class teachers and admins can read every subject for their class.

## Progress (tracking your students)
Open **Progress**.
- **Class teachers:** choose "My class" to see the whole class as a group: percentage by term, score spread, subject averages against last term, grades, attendance by week, who needs attention and who is dropping. Open any student to see all their subjects, attendance, conduct and trends.
- **Subject teachers:** choose a subject and class to see that subject as a group (average by term, continuous assessment against exam, spread, ranking, who needs attention), and open a student to see them against the class in your subject only. You do not see other subjects, attendance or conduct.
You can also open a student's progress from **Students** or from the score entry sheet (**View progress**).

## Report cards (class teachers only)
Only the class teacher of a class sees **Reports**, and only for their own class. Subject teachers do not see it.
Choose the term to see every student's report card.
- **Conduct and comments** (one student): rate the eight behavioural and seven psychomotor traits from Excellent (5) to Fair (1), or pick a **template** to fill all fifteen at once and then change any. A trait left as Not rated is left out of the total. Write the class tutor's comment, or pick a comment template (including one matched to the student's result).
- **Apply templates** (many students): choose everyone in the class or only the ticked students, then a conduct template and/or a tutor's comment template. Ratings and comments already entered are kept unless you tick Replace.
- Conduct templates: Excellent (all 5), Very good (mostly 4 with some 5), Good (all 4), Average (all 3), Needs improvement (all 2).
- **View card** shows the card as it will print. **Print / save as PDF** uses your browser's print window. **Print all report cards** prints the whole class, one page each.
The principal's comment is added by the school admin.
