# Parent and student guide

## Sign in and link your child
1. Ask your child's school for the **admission number** and the **access code**.
2. Click **Register**, create an account (email and password, or Google) and confirm your email. Choose **I am a parent or student**.
3. Open **My children**, press **Link a child**, choose the school's state, local government and school, then enter the admission number and code.

**One account, many children.** Press **Link another child** at any time to add a brother or sister, even if they go to a different school. Each child appears as a button at the top; tap a name to switch. When your children are at different schools, the school's name is shown under each name.

## What you see for each child
- **Progress:** charts of your child's overall percentage by term (with the class average), class position, every subject this term against the class average, one subject across the terms, attendance by week, conduct, and the strongest subjects and those needing attention. Charts also have a "Show the numbers" table.
- **Report cards:** the full report card for every term the school has published, to print or save as a PDF.
- **Attendance:** the weekly chart and the days missed. You can send the class teacher a note explaining an absence (a date and a reason). The note shows next to your child's name on the register. You cannot change the attendance marks.
- **Details:** update your child's family, health, emergency and address details, and religion if you wish. Only the school admin and the class teacher see them. Name, class, admission number and scores belong to the school and cannot be changed here.

Results, report cards and the progress charts for a term appear only after the school publishes the term. You only ever see your own children.

If the school revokes a code, that child disappears from your account until you get a new code.
