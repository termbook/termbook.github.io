One platform, three ways in

School admins

Register your school, confirm your classes, arms and subjects, then approve the teachers who join.

Teachers

Join under your school. Once approved, take attendance for your class, or enter scores for the subjects you teach.

Parents

Use the access code from your child's school to see attendance and results once the school publishes them.

Made for how schools really work

Free to use

No subscription, no hidden fees.

Phone first

Designed from 320px screens upward, with light pages for slow data.

Offline ready

Install it on your phone. It is built to keep working without a connection and sync afterwards.

Each school stays private

Teachers see only their own classes. Parents see only their own child.
